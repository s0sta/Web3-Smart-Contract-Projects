📘 How to use this portal

Every card links to a live dApp hosted under s0sta.com — each dApp also contains its own How to use, on-chain process explanation and flow graph.

  1. Pick a project card — difficulty stars rise from 02★ Foundations to 06★ Full Platforms. Beginners should start at 01 and move right.
  2. Click the card (or the → s0sta.com/… link) to open its live dApp. All dApps run on the Sepolia testnet.
  3. In the dApp, connect MetaMask and switch to Sepolia (the chain badge must show Sepolia Testnet).
  4. Read the How to use and Flow graph sections at the bottom of each dApp before pressing buttons.
  5. Get free Sepolia ETH from a faucet if a transaction asks for gas; the demo tokens used by each dApp are free and minted by the app itself.
  6. Want the source? Every project links to its folder in github.com/s0sta/Web3-Smart-Contract-Projects — contracts, tests, deploy script and the dApp.

⚙️ The process — how the learning path builds up

The 23 projects are five series. Each series builds on the previous one, from one smart contract to entire platforms.

  1. Series I · Foundations (01–05) — tokens, crowdfunding, multisig, escrow, NFTs: one-contract mechanics and wallet UX.
  2. Series II · DeFi (06–10) — staking, vesting, an AMM DEX, a DAO and a lending protocol: composable yield, swaps and governance.
  3. Series III · Real-World Governance (11–12) — Dubai's Owners Association (Law No. 6 of 2019) and DLD/RERA estate tokenization: statutory rules on-chain.
  4. Series IV · Islamic Finance (13–18) — waqf, VARA treasury, sukuk, takaful, murabaha and zakat: Shariah-compliant structures with committee governance.
  5. Series V · Full Platforms (19–23) — Mawarid (real estate), Tamweel (banking), Sahm (exchange), Rahala (payments), Huwiyya (identity): 9–12 contracts each, end-to-end.
