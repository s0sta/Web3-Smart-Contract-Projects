How it works

Sepolia testnet · what the buttons really do
🖱

How to use

  1. Connect your wallet with the Connect Wallet button (top-right). The dApp is configured for the Sepolia testnet, so switch your wallet to Sepolia first.
  2. Open the Invest card to check Your shares, Claimable rent and Your KYC before acting.
  3. Enter a share amount and press Subscribe to join phase #0 (100 AED-S/share). Your payment is escrowed until the phase closes.
  4. Once the manager finalizes the phase, press Claim shares to receive your allocation (oversubscriptions are refunded pro-rata).
  5. In The Exchange card, set shares and price per share, then press Place order to list your shares for sale.
  6. Press Claim rent in the Invest card for rental income, and use the Governance card to propose an appraisal or vote on proposals.
⛓

The process — what happens on-chain

  1. Every interaction starts at MawaridCompliance: it checks your KYC tier, sanctions status and per-asset exposure limits; a failed check reverts the whole call.
  2. MawaridPrimaryMarket.subscribe() pulls the AED-S payment from your wallet and escrows it in the phase; when the manager finalizes, allocations are pro-rata with automatic refunds.
  3. MawaridShares mints compliance-gated ERC-20 shares when you claim your allocation — every transfer re-runs the compliance check.
  4. In MawaridSecondaryMarket your shares are escrowed by the sell order; a fill pays the seller net and sends the platform fee to MawaridTreasury.
  5. MawaridRentalDistributor records rent, withholds the maintenance reserve and distributes each epoch using historical balance snapshots; holders claim their share.
  6. MawaridAssetGovernor runs token-weighted votes with per-type quorums and a timelock, while MawaridInsuranceFund covers rental shortfalls via 2-of-3 assessor approval.
