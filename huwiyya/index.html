How to use

NetworkSepolia testnet
Registry0x77701d73b4615715f3767E5ca308EB07Eb0A8Ae9
  1. Check the chain badge in the top bar — the dApp is configured for Sepolia; switch your wallet to Sepolia if needed.
  2. Press Connect Wallet (top-right) and approve the connection in your wallet.
  3. Press Create DID on the DID card to register your identity on-chain.
  4. In the Credentials card, fill the subject address and claims root, then press Issue credential.
  5. In Attestations & Reputation, enter a subject and a score (0–1000), then press Submit.
  6. In Gates & Governance, enter a proposed issuance fee and press Propose; vote For/Against on open proposals and press Execute when they pass.
⛓

The process — what happens on-chain

  1. HuwiyyaRegistry is the DID ledger: createDid() stores your key record and DID document, and supports key rotation, delegation, freeze and revocation.
  2. HuwiyyaSchema defines versioned claim-field definitions; HuwiyyaCredentials.issue() mints a verifiable credential holding a Merkle root of the claims (an issuance fee is collected by HuwiyyaTreasury).
  3. HuwiyyaCredentials.present() proves a single claim to a verifier with a Merkle proof built by HuwiyyaMerkle — the rest of the credential stays hidden.
  4. HuwiyyaAttestations.submit() records weighted attestations from trusted attestors; HuwiyyaReputation aggregates them into a decaying score and a named band.
  5. HuwiyyaGates.checkAccess() lets services gate access with policies combining credentials, reputation and disclosed-claim minimums.
  6. HuwiyyaGovernor is the ecosystem's parliament: propose() → vote() with reputation-weighted quorum → execute() after the timelock.
