📘 How to use Shifa

Shifa keeps the medical file with the patient and the paperwork on-chain. Connect a wallet on Sepolia, then follow the role you registered with.

  1. Register your role — pick Patient, Doctor, Clinic, Pharmacist or Insurer and press Register. Providers must be verified by the compliance officer before they can file records or dispense.
  2. Patients: grant consent — choose a provider and a scope (records, prescriptions, insurance or everything) and press Grant consent. Consent carries an expiry and you can revoke it any time.
  3. Doctors: file records — enter the patient address, pick the kind (lab, imaging, allergy…) and paste the fingerprint of the document. The medical file itself never leaves your storage.
  4. Doctors: prescribe — issue a prescription with dose, refill count and validity. Pharmacists dispense it with the patient's prescriptions consent; refills are consumed one by one.
  5. Patients: book and pay — book a visit with a deposit (refunded if you cancel at least one day ahead), then pay for care directly or settle your copay after an approved claim.
  6. Insurers: cover and review — subscribe members to a plan with a yearly limit and copay, pre-authorise treatments, and let two of the medical reviewers approve each claim.

⛓ The process — what happens on-chain

  1. Directory — ShifaRegistry.register() stores your role, region and license hash; the officer sets the KYC tier, and a frozen or sanctioned account can no longer practise or hold cover.
  2. Consent — ShifaConsent.grant(provider, scope, expiresAt) writes the patient's permission. Every Shifa contract asks hasConsent() before it lets a provider read or write.
  3. Records — ShifaRecords.createRecord() appends the fingerprint with author and timestamp; readRecord() reverts unless you are the patient, the author, or a consented provider — and it emits a read event so access is never invisible.
  4. Prescriptions — issue() caps controlled medicines, dispense() checks the pharmacy role, the consent scope, the expiry and the remaining refills before consuming one.
  5. Emergency — ShifaEmergency.openCase() mints a break-glass grant of at most 72 hours; a reviewer must later call review(caseId, upheld) to confirm or flag it.
  6. Cover & money — the insurer pre-authorises a ceiling, the provider files the claim, two reviewers approve, ShifaInsurance pays its share from the pool and records the patient's copay, which ShifaPayments.payCopay() settles.
