How this dApp works

How to use

  1. Connect your wallet — press Connect Wallet (top-right). The dApp targets Sepolia testnet, so switch your wallet to Sepolia first.
  2. Read The Property card: appraisal, share supply, your balance and your KYC status.
  3. Transfer shares: enter a whitelisted investor address and an amount, then press Transfer — both sides must be KYC-whitelisted.
  4. Pay rent: in Rental Distributions, enter an AED-S amount and press Pay rent — the dApp approves the stable token automatically.
  5. Press Distribute pool to open a distribution epoch, then Claim my yield to withdraw your pro-rata rent.
  6. Managers & regulators: use Management to file appraisals, whitelist or revoke KYC, freeze the property, spend maintenance and tune the reserve.

The process — what happens on-chain

  1. The manager registers the building in RERAPropertyRegistry via registerProperty() — name, appraisal and total shares create the on-chain property ledger.
  2. Compliance whitelists investors (setWhitelisted()) and the manager issues fractional shares (issueShares()); every balance is checkpointed per block for a provable history.
  3. Rent is paid in the AED-pegged MockStable through RentalDistributor.payRent() — the reserve ratio is withheld into the maintenance fund, the rest joins the pending pool.
  4. Anyone calls RentalDistributor.distribute() — the pending pool becomes a new epoch with a snapshot block and a per-share rate.
  5. Holders call RentalDistributor.claim(), which reads RERAPropertyRegistry.getPastBalance() at each epoch's snapshot block — shares sold later can never take earlier income.
