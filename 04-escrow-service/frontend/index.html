How it works (a guide for visitors · Sepolia testnet)

🧭

How to use

This dApp is configured for the Sepolia testnet — switch your wallet to Sepolia and grab free test ETH from a faucet first.

  1. Connect your wallet — press Connect Wallet (top-right); the chain badge shows the network you are on.
  2. Open a deal — as the buyer, fill in the seller address, the arbiter address and a deposit in ETH, then press Open deal to lock the funds.
  3. Release on delivery — as the seller, deliver the work, then open the deal and press Release funds to get paid (minus the platform fee).
  4. Cancel for a refund — as the buyer, press Refund on an active deal to get your full deposit back, free of charge.
  5. Dispute and resolve — buyer or seller presses Raise dispute to freeze the deal; the arbiter then enters the buyer's share and presses Resolve.
  6. Owner controls — if your wallet owns the contract, use Update fee and Withdraw in the Platform Controls panel.
⛓

The process — what happens on-chain

Every step below is a transaction on Sepolia that changes the state of the TrustEscrow contract.

  1. TrustEscrow.openDeal() — the buyer's ETH moves into the TrustEscrow contract, the deal is stored as Active, and the funds stay locked there until it settles.
  2. TrustEscrow.release() — the seller calls this on delivery; the contract marks the deal Released, credits the platform fee (feeBps, capped at MAX_FEE_BPS) to accruedFees and sends the rest to the seller.
  3. TrustEscrow.refund() — the buyer cancels an active deal; the contract returns the full deposit and charges no fee.
  4. TrustEscrow.dispute() — buyer or seller freezes the deal into the Disputed state, so no funds can move.
  5. TrustEscrow.resolve() — the arbiter picks the buyer's share; the contract pays the buyer, keeps the fee and sends the remainder to the seller.
  6. Ownable & ReentrancyGuard — only the owner can call setFeeBps() and withdrawFees() (pull-based fees), and every payout path is guarded against reentrancy.
